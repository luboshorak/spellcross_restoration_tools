Spellcross 0.7.9 hotfix4g - AI contact/defence response fix

Reproduction snapshot: divne.scsave
- enemy group 2: units 54 + 55 (Ka-orcs, type 38, fire range 1)
- group state: contacted=1, target_unit_id=67, order=STATIC_DEFENCE (4)
- positions: #54=(5,27), #55=(4,30), target #67=(5,22)
- result: group knows the player target but is outside its weapon range; STATIC_DEFENCE
  forbids movement, so it idles/digs while the player keeps shooting it.

Fixes:
1) PlanEnemyAIGroups(): the 0.5..0.8 strength-ratio branch now selects
   MOBILE_DEFENCE (5) instead of STATIC_DEFENCE (4). This preserves defensive
   intent but allows a contacted group to close only enough to return fire.
   Mission-authored fixed defence (SUICIDE_DEFENCE) is unchanged.

2) EnemyTurnStep(): MOBILE_DEFENCE no longer clamps its stopping distance to
   at least 2 tiles. Range-1 units (Orcs/Ka-orcs) must be allowed to close to
   range 1; otherwise they stop one tile outside their legal attack range.
   The existing MoveEnemyToward(..., true) logic remains responsible for
   indirect-fire minimum-range handling.

This hotfix includes the hotfix4f enemy-turn deadlock correction unchanged.
