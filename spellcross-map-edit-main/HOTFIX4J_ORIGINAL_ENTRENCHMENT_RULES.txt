Spellcross 0.7.9 hotfix4j - original entrenchment round rules
================================================================

Based on the original Spellcross manual and original JEDNOTKY.DEF data.

Changes:
- Entrenchment is finalized once per COMPLETE tactical round, after the Other Side
  phase and defensive/reaction fire, immediately before Alliance AP is restored.
- A unit gains digging progress only if it kept full AP and performed no action in
  that round.
- Defensive/reaction fire therefore correctly prevents digging progress.
- Incoming enemy fire no longer resets the defender's unfinished digging timer.
  The attack still removes one already-built entrenchment level, including a miss.
- Moving still removes all existing entrenchment.
- Firing/healing/special actions stop progress toward the next level but do not
  arbitrarily erase already-built fortifications unless the action already did so.

Preserved original data/mechanics:
- Per-unit rounds-per-dig-level comes from JEDNOTKY.DEF (field 0xB1/ENG shifted).
- Light units: maximum 6 entrenchment levels.
- Armored/other ground units: maximum 2 levels.
- Air units: 0 levels.
- Combat model keeps the reverse-engineered +5 effective defence per dig level.
- Every incoming attack removes one entrenchment level.

Not guessed in this hotfix:
- The original manual states that prolonged entrenchment/inactivity lowers morale,
  but the exact DOS numeric formula/rate is not yet verified. The existing morale
  TODO is therefore intentionally left without an invented constant.
